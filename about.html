Our story

Brewed in Auburn

From a garage roaster to your morning ritual.

How it started

Plains & Pour began in 2019 with a secondhand roaster in a garage on the edge of town. We spent our weekends handing out samples at the farmers market and our weeknights tweaking roast profiles.

A year later we signed the lease on a little brick building downtown. We've been pulling shots there ever since.

What we care about

☕ Great coffee

We roast in small batches every week and buy from farms we know by name.

🏡 Community

Open mic on the first Friday of every month, and our walls showcase local artists.

🌱 Less waste

Bring your own mug and get 25¢ off. Our grounds go to local gardens.
